# Clause map

What to compare between the MSA and the SOW (or order form, or amendment). Go through every topic the SOW touches. If the SOW is silent on a topic, the MSA applies: don't flag it.

## Does it belong to this MSA?

| Check | Look in | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| **MSA referenced** | SOW opening or definitions | No MSA named; another MSA; an older version; "the supplier's standard terms" |
| **Same parties** | Both documents' party blocks and signature blocks | A different supplier entity; the parent company instead of the buying entity |
| **Dates within the MSA's term** | SOW start and end, MSA term and renewal | SOW running past the MSA's end date; MSA already expired or terminated |
| **Order of precedence** | MSA definitions or "conflicts" clause; SOW opening | SOW says it wins over the MSA, in general or for named clauses |

## Clauses to compare

| Topic | MSA clause to find | Where SOWs usually deviate |
|---|---|---|
| **Price and rates** | Pricing, rate card | Rates above the rate card; new fees (travel, set-up, rush) |
| **Price changes** | Price increase or indexation | Increase cap higher than the MSA's, or added mid-term |
| **Payment terms** | Payment | Shorter payment terms; payment in advance; new late fees |
| **Invoicing** | Invoicing | Another invoicing entity or currency; invoicing before acceptance |
| **Expenses** | Expenses | Expenses not pre-approved; no cap |
| **Term and renewal** | Term, renewal | Auto-renewal the MSA doesn't have; a longer term |
| **Termination** | Termination | No termination for convenience; termination fees; longer notice |
| **Acceptance** | Acceptance, deliverables | Deemed acceptance after a few days; no acceptance criteria |
| **Change control** | Changes, change requests | Changes the supplier can make alone; changes billed without approval |
| **Liability** | Limitation of liability | A lower cap for this SOW; new exclusions |
| **Indemnities** | Indemnities | Indemnities removed or narrowed |
| **Intellectual property** | IP, ownership of deliverables | Supplier keeps ownership of deliverables; licence only |
| **Confidentiality** | Confidentiality | Shorter duration; one-way |
| **Data protection** | Data protection, DPA | New personal data processing not covered by the DPA; new sub-processors |
| **Security** | Security schedule | Lower security standards; exceptions for this SOW |
| **AI and data use** | Data use, AI | Supplier may use company data to train or improve models |
| **Subcontracting and staff** | Subcontracting, personnel | Subcontractors without consent; named staff replaceable at will |
| **Service levels** | SLA | Lower service levels; credits removed |
| **Governing law** | Governing law, disputes | A different law or court |
| **Insurance** | Insurance | Lower cover for this SOW |

## What the MSA usually requires of every SOW

Many MSAs list what each SOW must contain. Find that clause (often "Statements of Work" or "Orders") and check each item. Common requirements:

- Scope of services and deliverables
- Acceptance criteria
- Fees, rates or a fixed price, and the payment schedule
- Milestones and dates
- Named key staff or roles
- Term of the SOW
- Location of work
- Signatures from authorised signers of both parties

Only flag what the MSA actually requires. If the MSA has no such list, skip this check and say so.

## How to write it

One line per deviation, with both sources: "Payment terms: Net 45 in the MSA (clause 6.1), Net 15 in the SOW (section 5). Contradicts. The MSA wins (MSA, clause 2.3)."
