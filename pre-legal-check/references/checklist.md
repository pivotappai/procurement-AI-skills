# Checklist

Three parts: the information an MSA needs, the clauses to compare with the company's policy, and the checks for an order form or SOW that comes with it.

## Part 1: Information the MSA needs

If any of these is missing or unclear, the issue type is **Missing info**.

| Item | Check | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| **Parties** | Supplier's full legal name and the company's buying entity | The parent company instead of the entity that buys and pays; a brand name instead of a legal name |
| **Scope** | What's bought, or how orders and SOWs will define it | "As described in the proposal" with the proposal not attached |
| **Price** | Prices, currency, one-off fees, or how orders will set them | "Then-current rates", fees only in a schedule |
| **Term** | Start date, end date or length | No end date; a start date before signature |
| **Renewal and notice** | Whether it renews, for how long, and the notice to stop it | Renewal terms only in an order form |
| **Payment** | Payment terms, invoicing entity, currency | One invoice for several entities |
| **Signatures** | Signature blocks for both parties, with the right entity | Signature block for the wrong entity |
| **Referenced documents** | Every document the MSA relies on is attached | Terms at a URL, "DPA available on request", missing schedules |

## Part 2: Clauses to compare with the policy

For each clause, write what the MSA says and what the policy says.

- If the policy requires a clause and the MSA doesn't have it: **Missing clause**
- If the MSA says something different from the policy: **Departs from policy**
- If the policy has no rule for it: **Not in policy**, for Legal to look at
- If it meets the policy: don't list it

Only treat a clause as required if the policy says so. Don't add requirements of your own.

| Clause | What the policy usually sets | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| **Contract length** | Maximum length, or who approves longer terms | A long initial term, or renewals that add up |
| **Auto-renewal and notice** | Whether auto-renewal is allowed, and the longest notice | Renewal for the full original term; notice of 90 days or more |
| **Payment terms** | Standard payment terms | Shorter terms, high late fees, payment in advance |
| **Price increases** | Cap on increases during the term and at renewal | Uncapped increases; "indexed" with no index named |
| **Liability** | Minimum cap on the supplier's liability, and what's excluded from the cap | A cap lower than fees paid; data breaches excluded |
| **Indemnities** | Which claims the supplier must cover (e.g. IP) | Indemnity only from the company, not the supplier |
| **Termination** | Right to exit, notice, and fees on exit | No termination for convenience; fees due for the rest of the term |
| **Intellectual property** | Who owns deliverables and data | Supplier keeps ownership of deliverables or company data |
| **Confidentiality** | Mutual confidentiality and how long it lasts | One-way, or ends when the contract ends |
| **Data protection** | When a data processing agreement is required | No DPA, or one that's only "available on request" |
| **Data location** | Where data must be stored | Transfers outside the allowed region without safeguards |
| **Security** | Required certifications (e.g. SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001) | Certification "in progress"; supplier can change security terms on notice |
| **AI and data use** | No training on company data | Supplier may use company data to train or improve models |
| **Changes to terms** | Changes only in writing, signed by both | Supplier can change terms "by posting an update" |
| **Governing law** | Accepted law and courts | A law or court the policy doesn't list |
| **Sector rules** | Clauses the sector requires (e.g. DORA for critical ICT suppliers in EU financial services) | Missing audit rights or exit plans where the sector requires them |

## Part 3: An order form or SOW that comes with the MSA

Check that it matches the MSA. List each mismatch under **Order form or SOW**.

| Check | Watch for |
|---|---|
| **The right MSA** | Refers to another MSA, an older version, or "the supplier's standard terms" |
| **Same parties** | A different entity on either side |
| **Same prices and dates** | Different rates or fees; dates outside the MSA's term |
| **Terms changed** | New payment terms, auto-renewal, fees or a lower liability cap added in the order form or SOW |
| **Which document wins** | The order form or SOW says it overrides the MSA |

## How to write it

One line per issue, with both sources: "Auto-renewal: renews for 12 months unless cancelled 90 days before (MSA, clause 11.2). Policy: no auto-renewal above €50k (Contract rules). Departs from policy."
